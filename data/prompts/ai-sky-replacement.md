# Prompt Description
This prompt replaces the sky in a photo with a new one.

# The Prompt
```text
Replace the existing sky in the provided photo with a new sky.
Desired sky: {{new_sky_description}}
Adjust the overall lighting, colors, and reflections in the foreground to match the new sky seamlessly. Ensure a natural blend at the horizon.
```

# Documentation
Provide a photo with a clear sky, and describe the `new_sky_description` (e.g., "dramatic sunset with orange and purple hues", "clear blue sky with fluffy clouds", "starry night sky with a visible Milky Way").
