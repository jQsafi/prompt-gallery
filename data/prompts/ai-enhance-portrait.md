# Prompt Description
This prompt guides an AI to naturally enhance a portrait photo.

# The Prompt
```text
Enhance the provided portrait photo. Focus on:
1. Smooth skin texture without losing natural pores.
2. Brighten and add sparkle to the eyes.
3. Gently lift shadows under the eyes and on the face to create a softer, more even lighting.
4. Slightly boost overall contrast and vibrancy for a professional finish.
Maintain natural skin tones and avoid an overly retouched appearance.
```

# Documentation
Provide a high-resolution portrait image. The AI will return an enhanced version.
