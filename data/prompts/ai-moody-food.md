# Prompt Description
This prompt gives food photography a rich, moody, and appealing look.

# The Prompt
```text
Enhance the food photograph with a moody, dark aesthetic.
1. Deepen shadows around the edges, creating a spotlight effect on the food.
2. Enrich warm tones (reds, oranges, yellows) and subtly desaturate cool tones.
3. Enhance texture and sharpness of the food elements.
4. Add a subtle, soft vignette.
Make the food appear warm and inviting despite the dark backdrop.
```

# Documentation
Provide a food photograph, preferably with a shallow depth of field.
